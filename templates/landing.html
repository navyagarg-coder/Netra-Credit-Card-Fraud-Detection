How it works

From payment to decision in milliseconds

A payment arrives

Time, amount and 28 anonymized features enter Netra.

Two models check it

XGBoost gives a fraud probability. Isolation Forest scores how unusual it looks.

A decision is made

Low risk is approved, medium goes to review, high is blocked and queued for an analyst.
